IMPORTANT RULES OF CRICKET

1. Number of Players

Each cricket team has 11 players.

2. Overs

One over contains 6 legal balls.

3. Scoring Runs

Runs are scored by running between the wickets or hitting boundaries.

4. Getting Out

A batsman can get out in different ways, such as bowled, caught or run out.

5. Winning the Match

The team that scores more runs wins the match, subject to the match rules.
